What it adds

NWEA MAP

MAP compared with last May's STAAR, fall to fall, and across the summer, by student group. Each teacher's students on NWEA's five labels for every goal area.

District tests

DCBs, unit tests, CFAs, BOY/MOY/EOY. Teacher tabs built from the newest district test, plus a block for running test corrections.

One workbook

STAAR back to 3rd grade, MAP terms, and district tests side by side on each student's "class over time" strip.

Proof it works: on a synthetic showcase workbook (132 students, 6 teachers, every subject), scoring matched Eduphoria on all 26 tests and 3,117 records.
